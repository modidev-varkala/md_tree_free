about=Click-to-delineate tool for tree crowns on aerial or satellite imagery (GeoTIFF, XYZ or WMS). Click near the centre of a tree and its crown is outlined automatically: the plugin learns what the tree and the surroundings look like, ignores cast shadows, and fits a smooth round outline. Click and drag draws a circle by hand. Right click deletes a crown. Output is a polygon layer with an area_m2 attribute. Pure numpy, no extra packages.
